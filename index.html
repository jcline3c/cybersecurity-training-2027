What you'll learn

Cybersecurity in everyday situations.

Our 60-minute training uses practical examples to explain the security risks employees may encounter at work.

01

Phishing & Social Engineering

Spot suspicious emails, phone calls, and text messages, recognize common warning signs, and know what to do when something doesn't look right.

02

Passwords & Multifactor Authentication

Understand stronger passwords, safer password management, and why an extra layer of protection matters for your accounts.

03

Cybersecurity in the Workplace

See how everyday actions can affect security, from front desk teams handling payments and email to clinical teams working with patient information.

Cybersecurity is a team effort. The goal is to help everyone recognize potential threats, make informed decisions, and reduce the risk of a cyber incident.
